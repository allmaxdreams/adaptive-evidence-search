# Search Mode Gate Specifications

## Overview
The Search Mode Gate determines the appropriate depth of research required for a given query, avoiding unnecessary complexity for simple facts while ensuring rigorous, multi-layered investigation for complex or hidden phenomena.

---

## Mode 1: Direct Lookup
- **Trigger Conditions**: Single factual answer, single authoritative source, no contradictions, no causal explanation required.
- **Examples**: Luggage rules, event dates, official hardware specs, statutory tax rates.
- **Workflow**: Question -> Primary Source -> Freshness Check -> Answer.
- **Cost/Latency**: Minimal (single fast lookup).

---

## Mode 2: Structured Search
- **Trigger Conditions**: Information spread across multiple sources, ambiguous terminology, market/tech trends, comparison of multiple actors/approaches.
- **Examples**: Emerging trends in LLM fine-tuning, market shift analysis, reform progress tracking.
- **Workflow**: Research Question -> Light Ontology -> 2-3 Working Hypotheses -> Source Map -> Query Portfolio -> Knowledge Synthesis.
- **Cost/Latency**: Medium.

---

## Mode 3: Recursive Evidence Search
- **Trigger Conditions**: Partially hidden phenomenon, actors incentivized to manipulate/conceal info, weak signal detection required, high cost of error, conflicting sources, long-term monitoring foundation.
- **Examples**: Covert R&D programs, undisclosed supply chain bottlenecks, political coalition maneuvers, product maturity deception.
- **Workflow**: 
  1. Research Contract Definition
  2. Domain Ontology & Visibility Ontology Construction
  3. Competing Hypotheses ($H_1, H_2, H_0, H_V$)
  4. Traces & Anti-Traces Mapping
  5. Gemini Deep Research & Source Collection
  6. Atomic Claim Extraction & Provenance Tracking
  7. Evidence Criticism & Counter-Evidence Search
  8. Evidence Graph Construction
  9. Stopping Criteria & Calibration Review
  10. Multi-Level Knowledge Synthesis & Recursive Method Update
- **Cost/Latency**: High (Model-tiered agentic loops).
